                                          Broadcast Guidelines



These Broadcast Guidelines contain specific and mandatory instructions regarding the
use and broadcast of ESL Challenger League media content. They are part of the
obligatory License Agreement between EFG and Approved ESL Challenger League
Broadcasters and as such legally obligatory for anyone broadcasting ESL Challenger
League media content.




ESL reserves the right to revoke the permission of ESL Challenger League Broadcasters
to broadcast ESL Challenger League at any time.

Broadcasting
POV Streaming

You MUST add an additional 240 seconds stream delay. This is to prevent stream sniping and follow
the ESL requirement of a 240 seconds delay.


Delay

All community broadcast CSTVs are already delayed by 240 seconds, so no further delay needs to
be added.


Cleanfeed

EFG will provide clean feeds ONLY for matches that are broadcasted on the official ESL channels.
Any other ESL Challenger League match will not have a clean feed. The clean feed will already be
properly delayed by 240 seconds & will not need any further delay.

If a match is officially broadcasted by ESL, the channels that fall into the categories below, will not
be able to broadcast those matches:

     1. English language broadcasting channels
     2. All teams (and organization) broadcasting channels and
     3. POV Streaming (Broadcasters that play in ESL Challenger League and broadcast their
         matches)

If you are an English speaking broadcaster, and wish to cover a match distributed on our main
broadcast, please send an email with your Twitch or KICK channel to costreaming@efg.gg with at
least 3 day’s notice. Keep in mind that English watch parties are currently only permitted on KICK
and Twitch.

If a match is not broadcasted on the official ESL channels, all approved Media Rights partners and
community broadcasters will have the possibility to broadcast that match. This also includes:

     2. POV Streaming

All non-broadcasted matches are open to any language broadcasters and can be accessed via
provided CSTVs from tournament administration in the official broadcasters Discord.


CSTVs

CSTVs will be provided in the relative regional channels where you must put the Date, Match, and
your Twitch / Youtube / Other Stream Link if you are planning to broadcast this match.


Live

You should push your broadcast “Live” 15 minutes before the first game is due to start.

If you wish to broadcast something different before or after covering the ESL Challenger League
match(es) on the same stream, you can. However, always make sure to update the stream title.


Stream Title

LIVE: Team1 vs Team2 | ESL Challenger League | Season 52 | <Europe, North America, Asia, South
America, Oceania>

In the title make sure to always include ESL Challenger League or #ECLS52 as keywords.




Advertising
As an Approved ESL Challenger League Broadcasters you are expected to know what you should
and shouldn’t advertise on your broadcast. Here is a good practices list:

Do Promote:

     ● The Teams
     ● ESL, FACEIT, ESEA Leagues and how ESL Challenger League is one of the direct paths into
        ESL Pro League
