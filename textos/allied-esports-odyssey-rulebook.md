1. VALIDITY

This is the only rulebook for the Allied Esports Odyssey and all matches played within
the scope of the Allied Esports Odyssey. By participating the players agree to abide by
all of the Allied Esports Odyssey rules stated herein. The rulebook may be amended,
modified or supplemented by the League administrators at any time, in order to
preserve the spirit of fair play and the integrity of official league play. In special cases,
the league administrators can make decisions that are not included in this rulebook.

If a provision of this rulebook is or becomes illegal, invalid or unenforceable in any
jurisdiction, that shall not affect the validity or enforceability in that jurisdiction of any
other provisions or the validity or enforceability in other jurisdictions of that or any other
provision of this rulebook.

3. ACCOUNT GUIDELINES

Player Account:

    ● Any person holding a legal Riot Games VALORANT account and a
      challengermode.com account, that has not been suspended at the time of the
      tournament and has reached the age of 16, is entitled to participate in the
      tournament.
    ● Every player must be registered via the official tournament form
      (challengermode.com) and enter correctly his/her Riot ID.
    ● The participants will need a unique username that will be used to identify them.
      This username will be the Riot ID associated with the legal Riot Games
      VALORANT account.
    ● Username/ Nickname changes must be reported immediately to the league
      administration via Discord and has to be changed on the tournament plattform.



3.1. TEAM ACCOUNT

Roster:

    ● A team must consist of at least five (5) players and may consist of up to seven
      (7) players.
    ● Each match must be started by five (5) players.

<!-- página 5 -->
   ● When requested, teams are required to send us all needed information including
     but not limited to logo, social media accounts and any other requested
     information.
   ● Each team must fill out a team sheet before the main tournament starts and
     report it to the tournament management. This includes all personal data (full
     name, nationality & ingame nickname) of the players, a current photo (where the
     player's face is clearly visible), as well as his current game account for Valorant.
     All participants guarantee that the information provided during the inscriptions is
     accurate and complete. Any information that would be found to be incorrect
     would result in an immediate disqualification of the whole team.

Ownership of the team account: The team account belongs to the creator. The
creator of the team account is automatically the “Team Captain”.

Contact: Administrators may attempt to contact you for any reason via Discord. Make
sure to reply on Discord within 24 hours at the latest to ensure flawless league play.



3.2. CHANGES TO PLAYER OR TEAM ACCOUNTS

Nicknames:

   ● Each participant must ensure that his/her participant name or team name is not
     unlawful, illegal, harmful, threatening, harassing, abusive, vulgar, indecent,
     obscene, invasive of privacy, hateful, harmful to minors, racially, ethnically or
     otherwise objectionable to any other person or subject.
   ● Players who have an unacceptable name will be excluded from the tournament.
   ● In-Game Nickname Changes Player name changes are possible after a season
     has started. They must be reported immediately to the league administration via
     Discord. Player name changes which have not yet been authorized by the league
     management are not eligible to play.



Team-Names:

   ● Each team must give itself a unique name. Sponsor names are not allowed. As
     soon as a team has registered, a name change is no longer possible.
   ● If you have to use a sponsor's name please contact us on discord.

<!-- página 6 -->
   ● AE reserves the right to change team names if they violate the etiquette or
     trademark laws.



4. ELIGIBILITY TO PLAY

   ● Each player must be registered at the tournament page (Challengermode.com)
     and must have joined a team before the start of the tournament.
   ● A team can only use players that are registered as members of that team.
   ● A player (active lineup or sub) is allowed to represent only one team in the
     tournament.
   ● Teams that do not have five (5) eligible players at the start of a match day will not
     be eligible to compete. If a player drops during a map and does not return to the
     game, the game must be continued with four (4) players. For the next map a sub
     is allowed to play.
   ● Only players who have been granted the right to play by the tournament
     management are eligible to play. The right to play can be withdrawn at any time
     by the tournament management.
   ● All players must be from Europe, teams fielding players from regions outside of
     Europe will not be eligible to participate.

Using non-eligible players will result in the disqualification of the whole team for the rest
of the tournament and upcoming tournaments.



5. GAME SPECIFIC RULES – VALORANT

Since the tournament will be held using the VALORANT matches, the common Riot
Games VALORANT rules will be used.

5.1. Game/Client: The newest version of the game has to be installed by all players in
order to participate in the VALORANT IGNITION Series. Should Updates get released
during a Matchday the participants have to install them asap, to minimise delays. A
screenshot of the update procedure with time and date shown will need to be provided.
For the exact time and date participants will have to use the homepage
www.atomzeit.eu. In addition to this, participants will have to inform an AE admin over
the VALORANT IGNITION Series support channel on Discord. It's up to the
Administrators to decide if they want to re-host a match /day.

<!-- página 7 -->
5.2. Anti-Cheat: Riot Games has equipped VALORANT with tools to counter cheating.
The game integrated anti-cheat tool Riot Vanguard is necessary to use. Any
circumvention of the anti-cheat tool will be punished with the immediate disqualification
from the Legend Series and reported to Riot Games.



6. MATCH SERVER AND SETTINGS

6.1. Map: The official VALORANT maps “Haven”, “Bind”, “Split” and “Ascent” will be
used as the official maps in the tournament.

6.2. Limitations in Gameplay: Gameplay restrictions can be added at any time.
Reasons for such restrictions would be known errors or benefits from certain items,
legends, skins, etc or any other reasons at the discretion of the Allied Esports officials or
the game developers/ publisher.

7. MATCH PROCEDURE

7.1. Confirm Participation

Each player in a team has to “Confirm Participation” for the team to join the tournament
and be marked as “Ready”. To do this, each player in the active line-up needs to go to
the Overview page in a tournament and click “Confirm Participation”. If a team does not
do this in time, they won’t be seeded in the tournament and will not be able to join the
seeded, started and now on-going tournament.

7.1.2 Exactly five (5) members per team must confirm their participation in every match.
During a match, it is strictly forbidden to switch team members.


7.2. Match start

All matches of the tournament should start as stated on the website or as soon as the
previous match is over. Any changes of the time must be approved by the tournament
admins.

<!-- página 8 -->
The first matches of a day in all phases start according to the times entered in the
schedule. In order to ensure a smooth process, the match participants must be present
in full and ready to play 10 minutes before the actual match date. The following matches
of the day are played back-to-back. The next match starts appr. 10 minutes after the
previous one has ended.

All matches are broadcasted. Please wait for Admin confirmation to start the match.

Map vote and connecting to the server needs to be done in time. Players/teams
showing no real effort to do so, or even delaying the process on purpose may be
disqualified.



7.2.1 Time Zone

There spective times of the matches will be displayed on the Tournament website
under: All match times listed will be in Central European Summer Time (CEST). If you
are in doubt about the start time, ask a Tournament admin, to be on the safe side.



7.2.2 Pausing

The game may only be paused for the following reasons:

   ● Player drop
   ● Player disconnect
   ● Technical issues

Stoppage of play may be requested at any time for the above reasons. The team
initiating a pause must first call the pause in chat. The match can only be paused during
the buy time. A pause may not be called during combat, or once the Spike has been
planted. Player's may not move around the map when a pause is in effect and are not
allowed to shoot anyone. Once both teams are ready, the team who paused the match
will unpause it. Each team will have 5 total minutes of pause time per map. Players
pause the game by pressing the Esc key and selecting "cheats". Abuse of the pause
feature may result in punishment for the team abusing it.

<!-- página 9 -->
7.2.3 Disconnect


Players leaving the game must do so by pressing ALT + F4 to ensure that they can get
back into the lobby itself. If you experience a disconnect, game crash or other problems
you're allowed to rejoin the match lobby. If you're not able to rejoin the match, the game
has to be played out as a 5 vs 4.


A game can be restarted if necessary, according to the discretion of the administrators,
if only 4 rounds have been played. Only an administrator is authorized to initiate a
rehost.



7.2.4 Rehost

Teams receive 1 rehost per map. More than two rehosts results in forfeiture. In the
event that there is a mutual agreement to rehost for non team related issues, this does
not count towards the rehost total. (Example: Rehosting when both teams have high
ping.)



7.2.5 Match management

Admins can attend any match at any time without reason. An admin's friend request
must be accepted.

         ○ Marschau

7.4. Map veto

The voting process is carried out in the special match chat function on the
Challengermode platform.

The first named team (standing on the left side) will be called Team A and the second
team (standing in the right side) will be called Team B.

7.6 Forfeit or leaving during the tournament

If a team leaves the Allied Esports Odyssey, the team forfeits all prize money
accumulated for the tournament and all its players get banned until the end of the
tournament from upcoming Valorant tournaments in the reach of Allied Esports.



8. Game Coverage


Every player is allowed to stream their own matches until the main event and as long as
they are still a part of it. Therefore the player has to contact the administration before
the tournament starts and ask for permission. In case of non-compliance with the rules
regarding live streaming of the tournament, the concerned team could be disqualified.

A delay of two minutes is required if you want to livestream your matches. Streamers
must use "Allied Esports Odyssey" in their streaming title. For a chance to be featured
on our Social Media Channels please use @AlliedEsports and the Hashtag
"#AlliedEsportsOdyssey".

External casters in spectator mode are not permitted.

At the time of registration, participants allow Allied Esports to use his/her tournament
related content to create best-of clips and use it for the live broadcasts.

All Allied Esports Odyssey matches will be covered by the official Allied Esports Twitch
channel (www.twitch.tv/AlliedEsportsEU).




8.1 Player Responsibility

<!-- página 13 -->
Players cannot refuse to have their matches broadcasted, nor can they choose in what
manner the match will be broadcasted. The broadcast can only be rejected by an
admin. The player agrees to make sufficient accommodation so that broadcasting of
matches can take place.

8.2 Interviews

For every game, one player from each team must be available for an interview. The
team has to provide contact information for an interview in that case. The player should
also be available for a comment after the match.

8.3 Photo and Other Media Rights

By participating, all players and other team members grant Allied Esports the right to
use any photographic, audio or video material on their website or for any other
promotional purpose.




9. FRAUD / DECEPTION

Any attempt at deception will be punished. This includes match-fixing, betting fraud,
competition manipulation and bribery.

Ringers, Non-players accounts and account sharing: The players registered to the
tournament are the only players permitted to participate. You may only play with the
game account registered to the tournament on Challengermode. Ringers or account
sharing will result in disqualification.

9.1. Cheating: The use of illegal software to manipulate the game in any way is strictly
forbidden. A violation of this rule comes with an infinite ban from all Allied Esports
tournaments/Events/leagues.

9.2. Exploits & Bugs:

   ● Game issues and exploits are handled on a case-by-case basis.
   ● Gamebreaking exploits which allow you to win games will not be tolerated in any
     case.
   ● Glitching into buildings or other structures with purpose is strictly forbidden.
   ● Disappearing due to manipulating textures will result in an instant disqualification.

<!-- página 14 -->
Since the possibilities to exploit unfair advantages in the game can constantly change
through patches and other updates the assessment of bug using, glitches and game
errors is at the discretion of the admins.

Should a bug occur within the game, it must be reported immediately to the other
players & the tournament committee and the match has to be paused.

9.3. Stream Sniping: If a participant gets caught stream sniping or taking advantage of
stream sniping in any form, they will be immediately kicked out of the league, no matter
how far the team has progressed. Furthermore, this will result in a lifetime ban from
Allied Esports related tournaments.




10. COMMUNICATION AND SUPPORT

The Challengermode platform has both a team and a private chat function, you can
communicate with individual players or teams in the corresponding chat. To contact an
admin, you should primarily use the support chat or the discord.

10.1. Admins: Any person authorized by Allied Esports to conduct the Allied Esports
Odyssey based on its rules and regulations will be referred to below as the “Admin". For
the purpose of fairplay, any admin is allowed to decide their interpretation of the rules
and take proper actions. Instructions of an admin are to be followed. Ignoring them may
result in the exclusion of the team or players. Any verbal and/or written abuse of a
tournament official will not be tolerated through any medium. Violators of this rule may
be disqualified from the Tournament.

10.2. Support Channels: To receive assistance or request help from a member of the
AE Admin Team live support at Discord is the only option. Being available in Discord
during the league is mandatory to ensure and optimize the communication between AE
Staff and players during the whole time of the league. Teams are responsible for having
at least one contact person in the discord, who is also present on match days. However,
it is mandatory for all participants to join.

Please write us a short message on the general discord of Allied Esports with your team
abbreviation so that we can give you rights for your corresponding area.



11. REPORTING GUIDELINE

Any issues within a match or game need to be raised immediately. We will not be able
to make changes to any match result after a tournament round has been completed.

For a quick and easy processing of support requests, all participants are requested to
provide as detailed information as possible and to capture the important moments by
means of screenshots or video. Support requests should only be created by one player
per team. Multiple requests from one team on the same topic will not be processed. To
provide evidence in the case of a dispute, please use either a video hosting/image
hosting website and include the link(s) in the support ticket outlining your dispute.
Complaints must be submitted within 5 minutes of the end of the match via Discord. All
inquiries and complaints about the league must be submitted via the support system.
Any requests to the league administration, which are not handled by the support system
of the Allied Esports Odyssey, will be ignored.
