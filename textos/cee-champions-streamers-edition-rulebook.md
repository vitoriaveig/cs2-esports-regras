3. Matches

3.1. Map pool

The map pool includes the following maps:

     • de_inferno

     • de_nuke

     • de_mirage

     • de_ancient

     • de_anubis

     • de_overpass

     • de_vertigo


3.2. Best-of-One

In case of Best-of-One matches, both teams remove maps alternatively until one map is
left which will be played. Map veto will be randomly seeded. A knife round will be played
to decide starting sides.

<!-- página 5 -->
3.3. Best-of-Three
In case of Best-of-Three matches veto will be:

   ● Team A ban

   ● Team B ban

   ● Team A pick

   ● Team B pick

   ● Team A ban

   ● Team B ban

   ● Last map will be played as a decider.

Starting team to veto will be randomly seeded.

4.1. Team composition
   •   Teams must always have at least 4 players from the country in which they respective
       streamers live in, in the active roster

   •   Be mindful of your team composition during the match. The above stated rule applies
       to players participating in the match as well as the roster of the team.



5. Teams and players

5.1. Teams
   •   Teams can have up to 7-man rosters (5 players, 1 coach and 1 substitutes).

   •   If a team is not ready 15 minutes prior the official start of a match, it might result in
       a lose by default, if not agreed differently with the main organizer

   •   In the case of emergency teams can use a hosting player for the match only after
       confirmation of the organizer.

5.2. Players
   •   Players under the age of 16 are not allowed to participate in CEE Champions.

   •   Only players that are not VAC banned or banned on any competitive platform such as
       FACEIT or ESEA/Challangermode (but not limited to) are allowed to participate in CEE
       Champions.

   •   VAC bans that are issued in other games than Counter Strike or bans older than 3
       years are not taken into consideration.

   •   Players cannot play for two teams at once or host any team even if they already
       dropped out of the tournament.

   •   Players are not allowed to have someone else to play on their account, breaking this
       rule will result in disqualification of the entire team.

   •   Player nicknames must not include anything vulgar, racist or any other form of hate
       speech. Players will face severe punishment for using said nicknames which can lead
       up to disqualification from the tournament.

   •   All players should follow fair-play and should not show any kind of toxic or
       unsportsmanlike behavior. Hate speech during matches is strictly forbidden and can
       lead up to disqualification from the tournament.

   •   Please note that the assigned Coach cannot act as a substitute.

   •   During the main event, all team captains have to be available on tournament Discord
       during the whole event.

8. Pauses

8.1. Technical pause
If a player has a technical problem, he/she or his/her team is allowed to use the pause
function. Players can request technical timeout by contacting an admin with reason for
technical timeout. Text and voice communication between players and coaches is forbidden
during a technical pause.

8.2. Tactical pause
Each team is allowed to call for a tactical pause of 30 seconds up to four times per map.
Timeouts can be called via ChallengerMode standard ingame mechanics. Teams are allowed
to take all four timeouts at once by calling them all individually once the previous timeout
expires.

<!-- página 8 -->
Teams will not be given additional timeouts in the overtime, but the unused timeouts can still
be used in overtime.


8.3. Admin pause
The admin can pause the game from his station when it is required. If for some reason the
player pausing does not work, they must request the admin to do it.


9. Demos

9.1. General
All players must record POV ingame demo per map for the full duration of all matches without
exceptions. A possible knife round is part of a demo and must be recorded. Possible overtimes
must be recorded as well. In general, not working demos will be treated as missing demos,
although the final decision is up to admin. If the demo is incomplete, the admin will decide
whether it will be treated as a missing demo. Players must keep demos for the duration of
CEE CHAMPIONS: Streamers editions.

9.2. Requesting demos
Admin team is allowed to request a demo at any time.


9.3. Recording demos
How to record, stop and where to find a recorded demo.

   ● Type "record <filename>" (where filename is a name you choose, without brackets
     <>) in the console.

   ● This will start recording the match from your point of view.

   ● To end the recording you can either write "stop" in the console or just leave the game.

   ● The file is commonly saved under the folder path "
     \Steam\steamapps\common\Counter-Strike Global Offensive\game\csgo".


     10.       Forbidden actions

10.1. Cheating
All forms of cheating are forbidden and will be penalized by either disqualification of the
whole team. All matches in the respective tournament stage affected by this disqualification
will be overturned.

Any players with past Faceit bans for cheating are not allowed to participate in the
tournament.

<!-- página 9 -->
10.2. Self-Kills
Intentional self-kills by console, incendiary grenade, HE grenade or jumping off the map to
shorten the round time is strictly forbidden.


10.3. Non-default character models
Players are forbidden to use non-default character models. Multiple violations of this rule
can lead to match loss or even to team disqualification from the event.

10.4. Bugs and Glitches

   1. The use of any bugs, glitches, or errors in the game intentionally is prohibited.
   2. Whether these bugs affected the match and the subsequent actions, such as awarding
      rounds or the entire match to the opposing team, or enforcing a rematch, is
      determined at the discretion of the tournament administration.
   3. If any bug used is not listed here, it is at the tournament administration’s discretion
      whether or not a punishment is necessary:


● The bomb may not be planted in a location where it cannot be defused
● Planting the bomb in a way that its not hearable to the opposing team (in box, on a box,
etc.)
● Moving through areas where movement is not intended by the map’s design, such as walls,
ceilings, and floors.
● Utilizing teammates as platforms to gain an advantage, allowing player to see over, under,
or through solid objects that should not be possible according to the map’s design (e.g. wall,
ceiling, prop, box, floor)
● Disrupting map features with the use of items, for example, stopping the train on Overpass.
● Pixel walking. Players are forbidden to "pixel walk". This is defined as when a player is taking
a position on an invisible ledge which is not within the design of the map but where the player
is otherwise stuck.


We recommend checking with the administration team to see if any bugs are permitted. (Such
as. one way smokes, eg. movement binds)

10.5. Movement Binds

Movement binds that contain aliases are not permitted.

ILLEGAL BINDS example:

alias "-w" "checkw"
bind "w" "+w"
alias "_checks" "-back; alias checks"
alias "+s" "+back; alias checks _checks"
alias "-s" "checks"
bind "s" "+s"
