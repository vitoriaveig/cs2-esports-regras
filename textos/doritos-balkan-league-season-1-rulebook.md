1 Introduction

This document outlines the rules that should at all times be followed when participating in the
Doritos Balkan League. Failure to adhere to these rules may be penalized as follows. If at any
point dispute, claim, issue or any situation happens during the league that is not described and
proposed in the rulebook, the judgment, and the final decision are in hands of the organizer of
the league, Tiltproof.

2 General Rules and Definitions

Players under the age of 16 are not allowed to participate in Doritos Balkan League - DBL.

Every team that has qualified for the main event of DBL must have their team present on every
match day on the server Doritos Discord server and be ready to make an interview after each
match during the league.

Team and player names are forbidden to contain socially unacceptable and disrespectful words in
any language. All teams and players agree to behave appropriately and respectfully towards
other teams and players, sponsors, spectators, the press, the broadcast team, tournament
officials, and tournament administration and representatives in general, otherwise, they face a
penalty or a harsher sanction administered by the organizer.

No player or any other team member may take part, either directly or indirectly, in betting or
gambling on any results of any match in the CS:GO Doritos Balkan League.

The administration team is responsible for tournament communication, organization and
production, and will act as the main point of contact during the league. All communication with
League administration will be conducted through the official Doritos Discord server, where you
can contact admins with any question.

The organizer reserves the right to revise, adjust, remove, or otherwise change the rules outlined
in this rulebook. The organizer administration also reserves the right to make a judgment on
cases that are not specifically supported, or detailed in this rulebook, or to make judgments that
even go against this Rulebook in extreme cases, to preserve fair play and sportsmanship and
protect the sponsors rights and policies.

An example of Minor incidents is, but are not limited to, the failure to upload required match
media (demos, comms, etc.), general unresponsiveness, and inadequate team information
(names, IDs, etc.). Minor incidents will result in a penalty of reducing at least 10%, per incident, of
the overall prize money received by the team and with a reduction of the league points won.

Examples of major incidents are, but are not limited to, not showing up for a match, swapping
players, all kinds and levels of cheating, use of any kind of bugs, abusing texture bugs (surfing,
defusing bombs through walls, etc.), silent plants collusion, soft play, playing with a wrong game
account, harsh language (disrespectful towards other players, organization, sponsors,
intimidation, sexual harassment, etc.), and other. Major incidents will result in a penalty of
deducing at least 40%, per incident, of the overall prize money received by the team, and with a
reduction of the league, points won, and/or removing the team from the league completely.

If a team disbands during the league, all players will be banned to play in the next season, and/or
additional seasons. In case that team disbands the organizer reserves the right to replace the
vacant slot.

All rights in the final ruling of any kind of minor or major incident are in the hands of the organizer
administration.

The contents of support tickets, discussions, or any other communication with tournament
officials and administrators are deemed strictly confidential. The publication of such material is
prohibited without written consent from the tournament directors.

The nationality of at least three players playing at any given time on the CS:GO map in the Doritos
Balkan League has to be from one of the following countries: Albania, Bulgaria, Bosnia &
Herzegovina, Croatia, Kosovo, Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, Slovenia, Romania. All players
have to submit proof of nationality by sending copies of a passport or ID card if requested by an
admin. Failure to prove nationality will be penalized with a default loss and possible
disqualification from the tournament. The nationality of two players in the team competing in the
DBL can be from any country as long as his/her location does not affect the playability of the
game. All players have to submit proof of nationality by sending copies of a passport or ID card if
requested by an admin.

Team and player names can’t contain socially unacceptable and disrespectful words and must be
changed at admins request. If any team or player fails to comply admin team can disqualify them.

The team needs to nominate one individual who can be a member of their roster (a Captain) or
can be an external figure (a Manager), who will serve as their primary point of contact with the
league administration. This person will be responsible for keeping the team in order, for team
deliverables such as sending over team information, participating in media obligations, etc., and
will be held accountable by the administration for any irregularities in a team’s behavior and
performance.

All broadcasting rights of the League are owned by the organizer, and teams and players are not
allowed to stream DBL matches. This includes but is not limited to: Video streams, TV broadcasts,
GoTV, shout-cast streams, replays, demos or live score bots, and other. Players and teams are
allowed to stream their matches during the qualifiers if they are approved by the organizer.
Players and teams are allowed to stream their matches during the league if they are approved by
the organizer. Breaking this rule will result in disqualification.

All prize money should ideally be paid out 60 days after the DBL finals have been played.

4 Qualifiers

The League consists of 4 rounds of open qualifiers. An unlimited number of teams will be able to
participate in a single-elimination bracket which will be played in best-of-one (BO1) mode until
the finals (top 2) where it will be played in best-of-three (BO3) mode for the advancing slot.
Participants are not allowed to reschedule their matches in Open qualifiers. In each qualification
round, the winner of the qualifier will advance and will be awarded the participation in the DBL.

The qualifiers will be played out on these dates:

   •   26.04
   •   28.04
   •   30.04
   •   03.05




5 League format – Group Stage

The league will be formed with 8 participating teams. The group stage is played in a round-robin
BO1 format. After 7 days (rounds of play), the top 6 teams advance to the playoffs. If two teams
have the same win-loss score, the winner of their duel has advantage. If three or more teams
have the same win-loss score, round difference decides the final order.

Group stage play days are as follows:

   •   12.05
   •   13.05
   •   19.05
   •   20.05
   •   26.05
   •   27.05
   •   03.06



6 Map pool

The map pool includes the following maps:

   •   de_ancient, de_mirage, de_overpass, de_dust2, de_inferno, de_nuke, de_vertigo

7 Playoffs

The top 6 teams are going to form the upper and lower bracket, the BO3 double-elimination
bracket. The top 2 placed teams will be directly competing in the upper bracket finals, with the
bottom 4 forming the lower bracket. The finals are going the be played in the BO5 format without
map advantage for the team from the upper bracket.


Playoff play days are as follows:


    •   10.06
    •   11.06
    •   12.06


All matches must start according to the pre-agreed schedule. If a team is late more than 5mins, a
minor incident rule will be applied.

If a player drops before the first kill in the first round of the first half, then the match will be
restarted.




8 Prize pool

The top 4 teams at the end of the DBL will receive prize money for their performance during the
League. Money will be distributed as follows:

    •   First place - €3500
    •   Second place - €2000
    •   Third place - €1000
    •   Fourth place - €500
