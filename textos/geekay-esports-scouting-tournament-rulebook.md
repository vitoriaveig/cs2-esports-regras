5v5 Valorant – Geekay Scouting Tournament
           Official Rule Book

<!-- página 2 -->
1.PLATFORM RULES

1.1 Platform – Account

• All players are allowed to have only one (1) account. All information registered on your account
  needs to be up-to-date and to have the correct information.
• You are not allowed to share or use another person’s account. Your account is for your use and your
  use alone.
• If you face any issues with your account, contact admins or game officials beforehand.
• Ignoring any rules mentioned above will lead to suspension of your account(s) and tournament
  disqualification.

2.GAME RULES

2.1 GAME ACCOUNTS

• A player is allowed to have only one (1) Valorant account active on their account.
• You are not allowed to share or use another person’s game account. Your account is for your use,
  and your use alone.

2.2 TEAMS AND ROSTERS

• Each team’s roster must consist of 5 players.
• Each team’s roster will be allowed to have 5 main players and 2 substitutes. Once the tournament
  starts, you cannot replace any player.
• A substitute player may only be added before the Tournament Start.
• This player cannot be part of any other team’s roster which is participating or has participated in the
  same tournament.
• Match has to be played in the following format: 5v5.
• A player is allowed to represent only one (1) team’s roster in the same tournament.

2.3 AGE RESTRICTIONS

Each participant (Player) needs to be of the age of Sixteen (16) or older at the point of the tournament
start date.

3.MATCH RULES

3.1 Before the Match:

• Tournament Round will start as scheduled unless said otherwise. Some rounds might get delayed, for
  all the information please check our discord, where all information is current.
• Once the match starts, on the website you will see the nickname of the captain of the opposite team.
  The rest of the players will be written on Geekay discord.
• The tournament admins and officials will be responsible for creating private lobbies for each set of
  games (BO3 or BO5). Map Choice is given to one of the teams for the first game- the second map will
  be given to the opposing team and third map will be picked by the admins prior to the set of games
  starting in order to maintain professionalism and fair play.

<!-- página 3 -->
• An invite will be sent to the leader and the rest of the teammates for each match, players must
  ensure they are prompt and on time for each game played. Failure to follow a prompt schedule will
  result in penalties and/or disqualification.
• The server has to be set up as written in GAMEPLAY SETTINGS.
• All the players have to be in the lobby before the time limit goes down (15 minutes). If there are
  players missing after this time limit, take a screenshot and post it in the Geekay discord. The team
  which has less than 5 players after the time limit will be disqualified from the tournament. If both
  teams are missing players, then both are disqualified.
• Once all the players are connected to the lobby, Game Admins have to check all the players if their
  Valorant nicknames match the nicknames written on the discord. If there are any discrepancies, the
  admin will convey them on the discord by writing into the #Geekay Valorant 5v5 Tournament the
  screenshot provided.
• The reserves the right to join any match as an observer. If that is the case, team captains will be
  informed.
• Team captains of each team will have to choose maps and sides as described:

   For BO3s, each team bans one map, then each team selects one map and which side they will start
   on for the other team’s choice. The third map is chosen at random from the full map pool. The third
   map will be chosen at random by the tournament administrators + the team sides.

   For Bo5s, Team A begins by selecting a map, then Team B chooses which side they’d like for that
   previous choice, then chooses the next map. It then flips back to Team A to pick a side and map, and
   continues until four maps and sides are determined. The fifth map is, like the third in a BO3, chosen
   at random by the tournament administrators + the team sides.

• After map selection team captains have to write that their team is ready in the lobby chat. The game
  should not start before that.
• The match has to start at the latest 10 minutes after the tournament round started. Before starting
  team captains have to take a screenshot of the lobby to confirm with the game admins.
• All the group stage games will be hosted by the group server region via Geekay
• Primary tournament server is Bahrain. EU servers will only be accommodated during the group
  stages, in situations where players experience extremely high ping.

3.2 During the Match:

• If one of the teams will not pick all the agents in the character selection screen and the game goes
  back to the lobby, the game has to be remade. (via discord)
• In the case that the game started before writing ready from both sides, the team which did not write
  ready has the right to call a remake before round 1 of the game starts. (before the countdown in the
  game of round 1 reaches 0). (via discord)
• If any of the players are not connected to the game before the counter reaches 0, the team can call a
  remake and the game has to be remade. (via discord)
• If the game started on the wrong map, the game has to be remade.
• In order to do the remake, all the players have to leave the match. The next game will start as soon as
  possible. Both team captains will have to write ready again. If the team is not ready by 5 minutes

    the game has to be played in the way it started after the remake, even if the issues will occur. The

•   If a player disconnects during the game, the game has to continue and will not be paused or remade.

•   If the servers are acting badly for both teams or the game goes down, please report it to us in
    #Geekay Valorant 5v5 Tournament.3.3 after the match.
•   If the game is BO3 or higher, the games have to start as soon as possible between each other.
•   If you would like to report a player or the team, write in #Geekay Valorant 5v5 Tournament that you
    would like to make a report. Admin will take the conversation into personal messages. Do not write
    specific names or team names in Geekay Valorant Discord.

8.CHEATING

• It is forbidden to cheat, modify the game files, exploit bugs, and/or use any third-party application
  which would give an unfair advantage against other users.
• All cheats are completely forbidden to use under any circumstance while playing in any Geekay
  event. Using any cheat will result in bans and disqualifications. This includes, but are not limited to:

    1.   ESP
    2.   Radar hacks
    3.   Wallhacks
    4.   Speed hacks
    5.   Aim hacks
    6.   Hitbox manipulation
    7.   Teleportation (any kind of teleportation, which is not possible in the game)
    8.   The usage of a bug/bugs to gain an advantage versus your opponents
    9.   Any other mode of cheats that gives an unfair advantage to the player/team.

• If a team uses a player that has an active ban, the team will be disqualified from the tournament.
• Players with Valorant bans on any accounts within two (2) years are not eligible to compete in any of
  the tournaments hosted by Geekay.
• Geekay reserves the right to monitor any player, for any reason, during the tournament.

9.BETTING, GAMBLING, MATCH-FIXING, AND TEAMING

• Players, coaches, team staff, team owners as well as hosts and staff from Geekay are prohibited from
  gambling or betting on any matches in any Geekay tournament.
• All sorts of match-fixing and/or teaming are strictly forbidden and will be punished. This may include
  temporary or permanent bans.

11.BEHAVIOR AND SPORTSMANSHIP

• Show respect to everyone involved in the tournament. This includes but is not limited to players and
  staff.
• Treat everyone the same way as you would like to be treated yourself.
• Always show integrity while playing in every tournament, be honest and play fair.
• Do not use any discriminatory language, including but not limited to any language regarding
  ethnicity, nationality, race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, or personal beliefs.
• Do not use any extreme foul language, including but not limited to excessive profanity or language
  that is graphically sexual, grotesque, or violent.
• Do not make threats of real-world violence or other intended harm to anyone involved in the
  tournament.
• Do not harass, stalk, or purposely do things to make someone else feel uncomfortable or threatened.
• Do not share personal information about yourself or other individuals.
• Do not engage in, request, arrange, or offer illegal activities or materials.
• Do not impersonate other individuals.
• Do not spam, be it in text or Voice.
• Do not share your account, your account is for your use and your use alone. Do not grant access to
  your account to anyone else, and do not access anyone else’s account, even with their permission.
• Geekay reserves the right to, in special cases, override and/or remove, and/or add any rule stated
  above to guarantee fair play.
