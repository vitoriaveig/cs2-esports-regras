General Rules:

What is the rulebook?
The rulebook is a guide document which is valid to the players for this Esports tournament. The
admin team are the decision makers for all cases which may occur and are not written in this
rulebook.

Codes of Conduct:

The players have been given a great opportunity to grow a name in the community as a good
player. The players must conduct themselves in a reasonable manner, maintaining appropriate
and respectful manners to the players, production, staff team and the tournament in general.

Strict rules apply to all players and for those players that do not abide by the rules, disciplinary
or legal action including contract termination and referral to the local authorities will be applied.

Rules and Regulations
  1.1. PUBG Mobile
    1.2.    Game version:
           1.2.1.The players must use the latest update for the game.
           1.2.2.Emulators are strictly not allowed, anyone caught using Emulators will be
                 instantly disqualified.
    1.3. Map Pool Selection
         1.3.1. Erangel - Miramar - Vikendi - Sanhok
    1.4. Eligibility
         1.4.1. GCC And Egypt Only
         1.4.2. Any Player caught playing from outside GCC and Egypt, that team will be
                 instantly disqualified.
    1.5. Tournament Procedure
         1.5.1. 2 Days of Qualifiers (Offstream) with a Squad cap of 64, top 32 from the
                 Qualifiers will move onto the Top 32 (Streamed), top 16 from the Top 32
                 will then move onto the Finals and the Top 3 will win the Tournament.
    1.6. Substitutions
         1.6.1. Each team is allowed 2 Substitute Players.
    1.7. Match Breaks (For Stream only)
         1.7.1. There will be at least a 5 minute break between every match, An admin will
                 inform the players if there’s an extended break
    1.8. Change of Players
         1.8.1. A player cannot have another player filled in their position of the
                 tournament. Only the substitutes mentioned during the registrations can take
                 place of the Player.
    1.9. Server Location
         1.9.1. Middle East or EU
   1.10. Player Connections
        1.10.1. If a player gets disconnected in the start/middle/end of the game, the game
                 will continue after it gets started.
        1.10.2. If a player faced connection issues before the game and extended the
                 deadline or disqualification limit, The player has to provide a proper
                 proof to the admin team otherwise the player will get disqualified.
   1.11. Cheating and Scripts
        1.11.1. Any kind of cheating or in-game exploit is forbidden, such as using
                 programs to have advantage against other players. If a Player is caught
                 cheating or using scripts, his/her team will be disqualified.
   1.12. Match Interruptions
        1.12.1. If a match was interrupted for reasons beyond the control of the
                 participants(e.g server crash or player crash), the tournament admin will
                 pause the game if needed.
        1.12.2. If there is no access to the pause system, The game will be restarted.
   1.13. Use of Bugs and Glitches
        1.13.1. The intentional use of any bugs, glitches, or errors in the game is forbidden.
                 The usage of the following bugs is strictly forbidden, if any bug is used
                 which is not listed here it is up to the admin’s discretion whether or not a
