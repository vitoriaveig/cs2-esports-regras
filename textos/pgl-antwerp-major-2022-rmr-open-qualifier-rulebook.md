                          1. Format and Schedule

Europe
Amount of slots: 19 slots
4 Open Qualifiers.
Format : Single Elimination
The distribution between EU1 and EU 2 will be determined by ranking.
The ranking will be made once all qualifiers in the region have been completed.
The teams will be divided into groups according to the results of the qualifier:
    ● the 1st place (4 teams)
    ● the 2nd place (4 teams)
    ● the 3rd place (4 teams)
    ● the 4th place (4 teams)
    ● the 5th place + the Far East of Russia winner (3 teams)

1st qualifier starts using the world ranking as of 21/02).

of places at the qualifiers:

    ● the 3rd place (4 teams) 22-25
    ● the 4th place (4 teams) 26-29
    ● the 5th place + the Far East of Russia winner (3 teams) 30-32


Assign Qualifying Teams to Groups
Within each RMR, the highest-ranked invited teams are paired with the lowest-ranked qualifying
teams.

qualifying opponent.

                                   8. Team Rosters
RMR invites are sent to the teams of five players who earned an invitation from the most recent
Qualifiers. If a team used a substitute, an invitation is extended to the five who played in the last
match they won.
A team cannot adjust their roster between the Qualifiers and the RMR. However, they can
register a different coach or substitute player.
A team (team is understood to mean the 5 players in the roster) that have already been invited
to an RMR event cannot play in open qualifiers.


                                     9. Substitutes
Teams are allowed to have one substitute player during the open qualifying tournament. The
substitute player must be registered in advance. If a substitute player plays a match that leads
to securing a slot in the RMR, that player becomes the main member of the team. This roster is
obliged to take part in the RMR.
If a substitute has not played any matches during the qualifier, the team reserves the right to
register the same player as the substitute for the RMR.
The coach and substitute may be the same person.


                                      10. Coaches
Coaches may not be physically or virtually present to communicate with the players at any time
during the match.

                     13. Play Button/Ip Notification
Once this button appears you have 10 minutes to join the server. You can join the server by
clicking on the “PLAY” button or copying the “connect server ip” command into your CS:GO
console.


                          14. Warm-up/Match Start
Warm-Up: Match will automatically start when all 10 players are on the server.


                                   15. Match Start

The match will go into the knife round. Once the knife round has been completed the winning
captain needs to pick a side by using the “!stay”/”!switch” commands. If the captain fails to select
a side within 1 minute of winning the round the sides will be randomly decided by the server.




                               16. Communication
All communication with Live Support admins is expected to be in English. All communication in
game and on the website is also recommended to be in English.




                             17. Match interrupted
If a match is involuntarily interrupted (player crash, server crash, network cuts,…) the Live
Support Admin may decide to resume the match to a round before the issue was present to give
both teams a fair chance.




                                        18. Pause
A team may “pause” the game at the end of the current round or during freeze time to allow a
player to come back. A team can’t “pause” the game during the current round unless it has been
expressly authorized by the Live Support Admin. The maximum allowed pause time is 10
minutes total, once the pause timer countdown runs out you will not be able to pause and the
match will still continue.

                    20. Forbidden in-game actions
The following actions are strictly prohibited during a game and can result in your account being
suspended:
   ● Any form of external script is forbidden.
   ● Using bugs which change the game principle (i.e. spawn bugs) is illegal.
   ● Moving through walls, floors and roofs is strictly forbidden. This also includes
        sky-walking.
   ● Planting bombs so that they cannot be defused is illegal. This does not include plants
        where multiple players are needed to defuse.
   ● Players are not allowed to plant the bomb in a location where it cannot be
        defused, in a location where it is not touching a solid object, or in a location
        where it is not making the normal “beeping” noise
   ● Boosting with the help of teammates is allowed in general, but it is forbidden in places
        where the textures, walls, ceilings, floors become transparent or penetrable.
   ● “Pixel-walking” is illegal (sitting or standing on invisible map edges).


Exploits and binds

   ●   Players are allowed to use alias jump throw binds.
   ●   Players are not allowed to use objects to cover a bomb in a way that makes the bomb
       impossible to defuse
   ●   Players are not allowed to defuse a bomb through a solid object

                        27. Map Pick-Ban Process
Best of 1 Pick-Ban Process
1. The left side team (Team A) starts the VETO.
2. Team A - Ban
3. Team B - Ban
4. Team A - Ban
5. Team B - Ban
6. Team A - Ban
7. Team B - Ban
8. Remaining map will be played
9. Starting side is decided by the winner of the knife round.

Best of 3 Pick/Ban Process
1. The left side team (Team A) starts the VETO.
2. Team A - Ban
3. Team B - Ban
4. Team A - Pick
5. Team B - Pick
6. Team A - Ban
7. Team B - Ban
8. Remaining map will be played as a decider
9. Starting side is decided by the winner of the knife round for each map.
