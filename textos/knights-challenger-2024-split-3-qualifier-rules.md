1.     Background AND PURPOSE

Riot Games, Inc., the owner of the game VALORANT ("Riot"), has engaged Knights
Arena ("Tournament Operator") to operate the VALORANT Challengers NA
("Challengers") League in United States of America, Canada, collectively the
"Region", as part of Valorant Challengers NA. Riot has delegated to the Tournament
Operator the responsibility for developing these rules, which will apply to Challengers
and all of its games, matches and tournaments that are held in the Region
("Challengers Rules").

These Challengers Rules apply to and are binding on: (1) the individual (natural
person), entity and/or group ("Owners") who registered/qualified a Team to participate
in a Challengers League ("Team"), and (2) to each Team's players (“Player”),
managers, coach (“Coach”), owners and other representatives. A Team's players,
managers, coaches, Owners and other representatives are referred to as the "Team
Members."

These Challengers Rules are in addition to, and not in lieu of, the Valorant Challengers
Global Competition Policy ("Global Policy"). In the event of a conflict between the
Global Policy and these Challengers Rules, the provisions that are most protective of
Riot (as determined by Riot in its sole discretion) will govern. Capitalized terms used
herein and not otherwise defined herein shall have the meanings assigned to them in the
Global Policy, unless the context shall otherwise require.

These Challengers Rules form a contract between a Team Member and the Tournament
Operator and Riot is a third-party beneficiary of that contract.

**Each Team Member must read, understand, and agree to these Challengers Rules and
the Global Policy before participating in any Challengers League.**

2.     LEAGUE STRUCTURE

2.1.   Definitions of Terms

       ■    "Round"

            A Round is an instance of competition in a VALORANT map that is played
            until a winner is determined by one of the following methods, whichever
            occurs first:

             ●   Spike detonation
             ●   Spike defusal
             ●   Team eliminated before planting or defusing the spike
             ●   Round timer expired
             ●   Team forfeit

       ■   "Map"

           A Map means a set of Rounds that is played until one Team wins 13 Rounds,
           provided that a Map will continue past the 13-Round limit until a Team wins
           by a margin of at least two Rounds.

       ■   "Match"

           A Match means a set of Maps that are played until one Team wins a majority
           of the total maps (e.g., winning two maps out of three in a best-of-three
           ("Bo3") series).

2.2.   Qualifiers Format

       ■   Challengers Open Qualifier 1 Format

            ● Single Elimination Bracket until Round of 32
            ● 32 Teams will be re-seeded and switches to Double Elimination
            ● All matches are BO3
            ● All times are in EST
            ● 4 Teams qualify into Challengers
       ■   Challengers Open Qualifier 2 Format

            ● Single Elimination Bracket until Round of 32
            ● 32 Teams will be re-seeded and switches to Double Elimination
            ● All matches are BO3
            ● All times are in EST
            ● 2 Teams qualify into Challengers
       ■   Challengers League Stage 3 Open Qualifier
           ●     256 team open qualifier to determine two team slots for Stage 3
           ●     The top 32 teams are reseeded, then participate in a double elimination
                 best-of-three bracket
           ●     The Upper and Lower Final winners will be promoted into Challengers
                 for the Stage 3 Main Event
           ●     All matches are BO3
           ●     Only the final day of the Qualifier (Upper and Lower Final) will have a
                 broadcast.
2.3.   Seeding

       ■   Challengers Qualifiers Seeding & Grouping

           For   purposes   of   map    selection   and   match distribution,      seeding

           ("Pre-Tournament Seed") for the Qualifiers will be determined by a
           combination of holistic Team rankings based on previous tournament
           placements and judgment of Tournament Officials in the order detailed

               1.   Previous Riot tournament placements            (does   not   include

           Teams will be placed into the single-elimination bracket, with the highest

           When the top 32 Teams in the Qualifiers are established, the Teams will be
           reseeded by their highest acquired seed in the single-elimination bracket
           and placed into a double-elimination bracket with the highest-ranked Team
           (#1) placed against the lowest-ranked Team (#32), and onwards until all 32
           Teams have been paired.

       ■   Seed Stealing

           Seed stealing will occur during Stage 3, the Open Qualifier and Main Event.
           Seed stealing is when the winning team of a Match will move on to the next
           round in the bracket with the highest seed from their previous match up.
           Example: The #8 seed Team beats the #1 seed they will move onto the next
           round with the #1 seed of the bracket while the other Team takes the #8 seed.

           Seed stealing will be in effect from 256 onward till the completion of the
           event.

       ■   Final event placement seeding into main event GSL

           Premier-qualified teams will be seeded according to their qualification date
           and placement within to stage 3 from #11 through #13.
           The team that wins upper finals will take seed #14 while the runner-up will be
           seeded #15, the winner of the lower finals will be seeded #16.

3.     Team MEMBER ELIGIBILITY

3.1.   Player Age

       No Player shall be considered eligible to participate in any Match before their 16th
       birthday, defined as having lived 16 full years.

3.2.   Multiple Teams

       ■   Players and Coaches may not play for or contract with more than one Team
           at a time. If a Player or Coach enters such an arrangement or contract,
           Tournament Officials reserve the right to bar the Player or Coach from
           playing any future matches until the situation has been resolved and the
           Player or Coach complies with the requirements in this Section.

            ●    As an exception to the above, Players & Coaches eligible to compete
                 in VALORANT Champions Tour Game Changers (“GC”) may play
                 for a Team participating in GC and a Team participating in
                 Challengers but not if both teams are participating in the same
                 event/tournament. These Teams may be separate entities or the same
                 Team participating in both competitions.

       ■   The VALORANT Concurrent Ownership Policy prohibits an owner or
           operator of a VALORANT team from having ownership or control of, or
           undue influence over, more than one team that competes in an Official
           Competition.

            ●    As an exception, an Organization may own a Game Changers Team in
                 addition to another second Team that competes in the VCT or
                 Challengers Leagues.

3.3.   Resident Defined

       A Player is considered a “Resident” if the Player is either (i) a citizen, lawful
       permanent resident, or the holder of other special status (e.g., refugee or asylum
       status) in the United States of America or Canada, collectively the “Region”,
       based upon the legal status in the Region.

3.4.   Roster Residency Requirement.

       In order to maintain the regional identity of Teams that compete in a global
       competition and to encourage the type of regional identification that is important
       to fans and sponsors, each Team must maintain, at all times during any Official
       Competition, at least three (3) Players on its Starting Roster that are Residents of
       the Region of Challengers North America.

3.5.   Proof of Residency.

       In order to be certified as a Resident, Players must prove lawful permanent
       residency in the Region.

3.6.   Single Residency Status.

       A Player may only be a Resident of a single region at any point in time,
       regardless of whether that Player has lawful resident status in multiple Regions.
       Any Player who relocates to a new region, will remain a resident of his, her, or
       their prior region until the one-year anniversary of their relocation.

3.7.   No Riot or Knights Employees.

       Team Members may not be employees of the Pittsburgh Knights, LLC or Riot
       Games Inc. ("RGI"), North America League of Legends Championship Series
       LLC ("NALCS LLC"), the League of Legends eSports Federation LLC or any of
       their respective affiliates at the start of or at any point during the 2023 Valorant
       Challengers NA League. "Affiliate" is defined as any person or other entity
       which owns or controls, is under the ownership or control of, or is under
       common ownership or control with, an owner. "Control" shall mean the power,
       through any means, to determine the policies or management of an entity,
       whether through the power to elect, appoint or approve, directly or indirectly, the
       directors, officers, managers or trustees of such entity or otherwise.

5.     ROSTER RULES
5.1.   Ringing - Stage 3 Restrictions

       Any Player who has signed up and participated in the Qualifier will not be allowed
       to play in any manner for another Team in the same event they have participated
       in.

       Teams Entering Stage 3 from Open Qualifier & Premier Moveups

        A premier team or an Open Qualifier team that qualifies for Stage 3 via the
       Premier Season or the Open Qualifier event, the team is required to maintain all 5
       of their starting players who played in the respected qualification event that earned
       them the slot. Teams who do not maintain this requirement will be replaced by
       another Team in Challengers.

5.2.   Admin Discretion

       Any roster changes including emergencies during tournament is case by case and

       under the sole discretion of the Tournament Operator.

5.3.   Coaches

       If a Team has a coach, then that coach may be present for every Match in which
       the Team participates. For Challengers Open Qualifier, Teams will be allowed to
       register a second coach (any staff member of Team) who can take the place of
       the coach at the Team’s discretion as long as they are registered with the league.
       For Challenger Open Qualifier matches, both of a Team's designated coaches
       may be connected to the voice communication system with the team and will
       only be allowed to talk to Players and others during the Agent and Map selection
       process for each Match, timeouts, half-time buy phase, overtime buy phase and
       in between Maps.

5.4.   Emergency Substitutions

       In the event of an emergency at any point during a Match, a Team will be given
       up to twenty minutes to produce an eligible Substitute. Tournament Officials will
       determine if an incident qualifies as an emergency and reserve the right to rule
       on each emergency on a case by case basis.

5.5.   Qualified Team Rules

       Teams who qualify for Challengers will need to maintain 3 of 5 Players who
       started a minimum of 75% of Teams matches in the qualifier. Teams who do not
       maintain this requirement will be replaced by another Team in Challengers

       Qualified Teams must submit their Challengers rosters before October 27th,
       2024 at 11:59 PM Local Time. The Team must designate five (5) Starters which
       shall constitute the Team's "Starting Roster." Rosters are considered public at
       the submission deadline. At this time, two (2) Substitute Players may also be
       designated as eligible to replace a Starter in Challengers in the event the Starting
       Player is unable to compete or at Team discretion before each Map.

       Matches within a given day and/or change the date of a Match to a different date

       Officials modify a Match schedule, they will notify all Teams as soon as
       possible.

■   Map Selection Process for Best-of-Three Matches.

    The higher seeded Team will decide whether they are Team A or Team B,
    with the highest seed being 1 and the lowest being 256. Seed-stealing will
    be implemented. Seed-stealing is when the winning team of a Match will
    move on to the next round in the bracket with the highest seed from their
    previous match up. Example: The 32nd seed Team beats the #1 Seed they
    will move onto the next round with the #1 seed of the Qualifier while the
    other Team takes the #32 seed. Once the higher-seeded team has made their
    choice, Team A starts the process, and the map for the Match will be
    selected according to the following procedure:

    ●    Team A removes one map from the map pool.
    ●    Team B removes one map from the map pool.
    ●    Team A selects the map for the first map of the best-of-three Match
         and Team B selects their starting side.
    ●    Team B selects the map for the second map of the best-of-three Match
         and Team A selects their starting side.
    ●    Team A removes one map from the map pool.
    ●    Team B removes one map from the map pool, leaving the final
         remaining map to be played in the best-of-three Match. Team A will
         select their starting side.

7.     PAUSES AND CRASHES
7.1.   Pauses/Timeouts

       There are a total of (2) TWO Tactical Pause and (1) ONE Technical Pause per
       MAP.

       Tactical Pauses: Teams are allowed to call In Game Timeouts of 60 seconds in
       duration two times per map not limited by half. Timeouts can be called via the
       in-game pause system. In the event of overtime Teams are granted 1 additional
       Timeout.

       Technical Pauses: If a Player has a problem that prevents them from playing on,
       they must notify the Tournament Official and their opponent. The Player has to
       announce the reason before or immediately after the pause. During a technical
       pause, headsets have to stay on and Players are prohibited from controlling their
       agent (i.e., Players can not move or use utility during a technical pause).

       Maximum of 5 Minutes total throughout the map using the Pause Match feature
       (If the technical issue cannot be resolved during the 5 minute timer and doesn't
       have an imminent resolution Tournament Officials may escalate to the next level
       and grant a 20 minute timer).

7.2.   Crashes

       ■   In the event that a Player disconnects during a map and is unable to return
           within the allocated pause time, the Team will be permitted twenty (20)
                                                                                       15

<!-- página 16 -->
    minutes to replace them with a Substitute from their Roster for the next map
    if applicable. Any further disconnections will be reviewed and ruled on by
    the Tournament Operator in their sole discretion. Any substitutions must be
    on the approved Qualifier roster prior to the game.

■   In the case where a Player is unable to continue during a Map or a Match,
    and there are no pre-registered Substitutes on the roster, Teams are allowed
    twenty (20) minutes to register an Emergency Substitute, this is on a case by
    case basis at the sole discretion of a Tournament Operator.

■   If a match is interrupted for any technical reason (e.g. server or Player
    crash), the Players may implement a Technical Pause and inform a
    Tournament Administrator as soon as possible. Once the Technical Pause
    has expired and the issue has not been resolved, the game must resume.
    Additional time for the Technical Pause may be granted per the Tournament
    Officials’ discretion.

■   If the issue takes place during the first Round of the Match, before any
    damage has occurred and the opponent or referee has been immediately
    notified, the Match will be restarted.

■   If a match is interrupted for reasons beyond the control of the Players (e.g.
    server or Player crash), Tournament Officials, if possible, will restore the
    Round using the in-game Round restore feature, but in some scenarios may
    decide to replay the Round or Match.

■   If a match is interrupted for any technical reason (e.g. broken peripherals),
    the Players can implement a Technical Pause and inform a Tournament
